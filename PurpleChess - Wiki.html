How to Join

Last updated: January 2027

Follow these simple steps to join PurpleChess and start your adventure!

PurpleChess App: With the Alpha 2.5 Release

  1. 1

    Media Page:

    Visit the PurpleChess media page for the download link.

  2. 2

    Download the App

    Click the download link on the media page to get the latest version.

  3. 3

    Allow Permissions

    Click Download and allow permissions. This may include but is not limited to notifications, and in future updates cammera.

    Camera updates may include video calls and/or Discord integration.
  4. 4

    Update, Buy and Join!

    You can join the club, launching Chess.com from the link on the Play page. You can also redeem points and purchase items right from the app!

Purple Chess Website:

  1. 1

    Open the Play Page

    Go to the PurpleChess website and navigate to the Play page.

  2. 2

    Click the Launch Button

    From the Play page, click the launch button to open Chess.com.

  3. 3

    Create a Chess.com Account then join PurpleChess

    Click the "Create Account" button on the Chess.com website if no account exists and follow the instructions to create your account. Once created, you can join the PurpleChess server at:

    https://www.chess.com/club/purplechess
  4. 4

    Play and Buy!

    Once you're in the game, you can play and purchase items from the website's store with PurpleCoins!

Current Website Version

Current website version is Alpha 1.0.0. Updates are released periodically.

PurpleChess Club Rules

Last updated: January 2027

Please read and follow these rules to ensure a great experience for everyone.

PurpleChess Chess.com Rules

1

Spamming

Sending 4+ repeated or similar messages results in a mute.

2

Racism & Hate Speech

Any racial remarks or hate speech lead to long mutes and eventual bans.

3

Hacking & Cheating

Illegal clients, hacked clients, bots, or unfair JavaScript is not allowed.

4

Cheating Encouragement

Messages promoting cheating are strictly prohibited.

5

Foul Language

Replacing letters with numbers or symbols to bypass filters is not allowed. Keep the standards high.

6

Threats

Threatening to hack or otherwise inconvenience players is not allowed.

7

Chargebacks

Any chargeback on store purchases results in a permanent ban.

8

Exploiting

Abusing bugs or exploits results in a ban. Report issues to staff instead.

9

Game Throwing

Creating Alts, or throwing games in any way is not allowed.

10

PurpleCoin False Spending

Spending someone elses PurpleCoins results in an instant ban.

11

Board Exploits

Board or TE exploits may result in immediate bans.

12

Impersonation

Pretending to be another player or staff is not allowed.

13

Admin Bribery

Attempting to influence or bribe staff members is not allowed.

Teams Chat Rules

1

Spamming

Hacking Guidelines

Definition

Hacking is prohibited. It requires exploiting game mechanics, whether backend or frontend, and lacks of consent of personA(s) or Chess.com.

Not Considered Hacking

Exploiting game mechanics in a way that does not violate the terms of service, such as Openings or Reviews.

Evidence Requirement

Video recordings are required for all Hacking reports.

CommunityFAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: January 2026

Answers to commonly asked questions about PurpleChess.

How do I join the actual Chess.com Club?

Go to https://www.chess.com/club/purplechess and submit a request to join the club, with your full name and a brief description of your chess experience. You will be notified when your request is approved.

Is there an app for PurpleChess?

Not yet, but we are considering developing one in the future.

What is the line for hacking and not hacking?

Chess.com has a strict policy against hacking and cheating. Also check our rules on it under the rules section.

How do I report a hacker?

Message an Administrator. Video evidence is required.

Do you have an economy?

Yes, we have Purple Chess Points (PCP). You can buy perks with PCP to stand out in the club.

How can I earn Purple Chess Points?

You can earn Purple Chess Points by participating in tournaments and playing games. Tournament games offer more points than regular games.

Who are the club Administrators?

The Club Administrators are a group of trusted members who help manage the club and enforce the rules. You can see the list of administrators on the Chess.com club page.

Still have questions?

Join our Teams Chat and ask our friendly community or staff!
