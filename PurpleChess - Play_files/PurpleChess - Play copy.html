Quick Start Guide

1

Launch the Client

Click the "Launch Game" button above to open the Tuff Client in a new browser tab.

2

Set Your Username

Enter your desired username when prompted. This will be your in-game name.

3

Go to Multiplayer

From the main menu, click "Multiplayer" to access the server list.

4

Add the Server

Click "Add Server" and enter the server address:

wss://mc.voidsent.net
5

Join & Play!

Select Voidsent from your server list and click "Join Server" to start playing!

Controls

W A S D Movement
Space Jump
Shift Sneak
E Inventory
Left Click Attack/Break
Right Click Use/Place
Q Drop Item
T Chat
Esc Pause Menu
F5 Toggle View

Tips

Use a modern browser like Chrome or Edge for the best performance.

Click on the game window to capture your mouse for better controls.

Press Esc to release your mouse from the game.

For the best experience, play in fullscreen mode (F11).

Have Java or Bedrock Edition?

You can also connect directly using play.voidsent.net
