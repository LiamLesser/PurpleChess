Quick Start Guide

1

Launch Chess.com and Find PurpleChess

After signing up or logging in, go to clubs and search for PurpleChess.

2

Join PurpleChess

Click "Join Club" and enter your full name and how you know about PurpleChess.

3

Join the Teams Chat

Join the Teams chat to connect with other players and communicate with staff.

4

Find a Tournament

Navigate to the "Tournaments" section on Chess.com next to the Chat.

5

Play!

Join a tournament and start playing to earn PurpleChess Points!

Tips

Use Brave Browser to block ads in gameplay.

Join a tournament a little early to prepare and find what kind it is.

Press the four arrows to fullscreen, or press Esc to go to normal view.

For the best experience, play in fullscreen mode.

Playing on Chess.com App:

Basically the same as playing on the website, but in a mobile-friendly interface. Make sure that you are not using the browser version on a mobile device.
