Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to commonly asked questions about PurpleChess.

Club Information

What is the link to join PurpleChess?

The server's IP address is https://www.chess.com/club/purplechess.

Is there an app for PurpleChess?

No, not currently. Developers are looking into creating one in the future.

Gameplay

What is hacking?

Hacking refers to the act of exploiting bugs or glitches in the game to gain an unfair advantage or to compromise the game's integrity.

Is there a custom economy for PurpleChess?

Yes, we have a custom economy system with its own currency called PurpleChess Points (PCP).

Economy

What are PurpleChess Points (PCP) for?

PurpleChess Points (PCP) can be used to purchase items, services, and exclusive content within the PurpleChess community.

How do I earn PurpleChess Points (PCP)?

You can earn PurpleChess Points (PCP) by participating in club tournaments and playing games.

Support

How do I report issues or rule-breaking players?

Report players to our staff in the Teams Chat. Create a ticket and provide evidence of the violation.

How can I join the Teams community?

Join our Teams Chat to chat with other players, get support, and stay updated on server news and events!

How do I apply for staff?

Staff applications are handled through the forms on the Apply page of the website.. Check the announcements channel for information on when applications are open.

Still have questions?

Join our Teams Chat and ask our friendly staff or players!
