How Ranking Works

Rank badges come from total points, not just leaderboard position.

1

Each mode tier gives a fixed amount of points. Higher tiers are worth more.

2

Your total points are added together across all recorded modes.

3

When your total reaches a threshold, your combat rank upgrades automatically.

4

Mode pages show mode-specific tiers, while the overall page sorts by total points.
